CORE: When asked to challenge a pretrial ruling, analyze rather than reteach. NEVER assume every adverse ruling is directly appealable. Classify the ACTUAL order: tentative vs minute vs signed order vs judgment, dispositive vs interlocutory, and limited vs unlimited civil. Reconstruct entries and service notices from the record.

IMMEDIATE ROUTE COMPARISON (WITH RELEVANCE, GROUNDS, DEADLINE TRIGGERS, RECORD GAPS):
1) DIRECT APPEAL — verify exact statutory appealability under CCP 904.1 (e.g., injunction rulings, final judgment; classify TRO separately). Distinguish an order sustaining demurrer from a judgment of dismissal, or an order granting summary judgment from entered judgment. Verify exceptions and statutory conditions for anti-SLAPP and sanction orders.
2) EXTRAORDINARY WRIT — determine discretionary/statutory mandate or prohibition and irreparable injury, absence of adequate remedy later, need for prompt action and adequate record; verify special windows, including CCP 437c(m). A writ filing does not automatically stay proceedings. CRC 8.486 addresses original writ record and urgent stay requests.
3) RECONSIDERATION — CCP 1008(a): verify new/different facts, circumstances or law, original order and written notice of entry; generally 10 days after qualifying service. Do not reuse the same argument and call it new. Denial of reconsideration is not independently appealable under §1008(g).
4) RENEWED MOTION — CCP 1008(b): genuinely new/different material or different permissible relief, not a renamed repeat. Test motion-specific rules.
5) PRESERVE FOR APPEAL — CCP 906: later review of qualifying intermediate rulings on appeal from a judgment, excluding orders that could themselves have been appealed. Audit trial-court record, objection, prejudice, standard of review, remedies.
6) STAY / EXPEDITED RELIEF — test CCP 916 and exceptions, and specific stay requirements. Never state that appeal automatically halts the entire case or that a writ itself grants a stay.
7) CLOCK TRIAGE — distinguish date order rendered/entered, qualifying notice of entry or endorsed copy served with proof, filing method, relevant statutory special periods, CRC 8.104 and 8.108. Rule 8.104 generally earliest qualifying 60-day service period or 180-day entry period, subject to exceptions. A reconsideration motion does NOT automatically toll time: CRC 8.108(e) applies only under its conditions, including a valid motion concerning an appealable order. Do not calculate unverified definitive dates.
8) MERITS / APPELLATE STRATEGY — find reviewable legal error, standard of review, trial record citations, forfeiture, prejudice/harmless error, opposing counsel's strongest response, alternative remedy and minimum viable next move. Do not confuse adverse ruling with legal error.
