How to test it

1. Download

Save the plain-text instruction file linked above. It is not an automatically installed ChatGPT plugin.

2. Start a fresh chat

Upload the instructions and authorized documents to your own ChatGPT account. Check your account’s data settings first.

3. Request full review

Ask for a document inventory, decisive elements, evidence gaps, procedure and citations checks, counterarguments, and overlooked issues.

Suggested first prompt

Use the attached Legal Strategist beta instructions. Inventory all documents and pages actually read; identify missing material. Verify the court, procedural status, service and deadlines, and the decisive elements. Map every material claim to its original source. Conduct separate adversarial, judicial, evidence-skeptic, connections, and strategy reviews. Find the five most consequential overlooked issues. Check the original full holdings and dispositions of cited authorities where available, identifying everything unverified. Do not draft court papers until I approve an elements checklist and specifically authorize the document.

Current limits

This is experimental legal research assistance, not an attorney or court-approved system. It does not provide guaranteed correctness, complete authority verification, connected legal databases, automatic cross-chat memory, or direct control over Claude/ChatGPT sessions. Independent verification and human judgment remain necessary.

Privacy: Do not post identifiable case documents, medical records, privileged material, or private communications to GitHub issues or this public repository. The static site does not receive uploaded documents. ChatGPT handles files you choose to submit under its own policies and account settings.
